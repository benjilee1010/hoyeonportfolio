Design consulting · GovTech

Ascade

Design consulting for the government page of Ascade, a security company whose product is built on an seL4-based firewall.

Role
Design consultant
Scope
Advising on the design, not building the page
Page
ascade.co/gov

Context

What the page is for

The page introduces Ascade's secure enclaves to government teams and defense integrators: isolated environments with precise control over workloads, storage and network access.

My role was to advise on the design. It was the same kind of work as on TerpTax, but I wasn't building the page.

How I worked

Talking it through, then drawing it

I walked the founder through the pages on a call and explained what I would change and why. I also drew directly over the existing site in drawing apps, so each note came with a visual instead of only a description.

Design decisions

The design should match the product

The principle is the one I used on TerpTax: a site should reflect what the company is. Ascade's product is technical and security-focused, so I pushed for hard corners and a precise, exact look.

The other problem was structure. Each section had to be easy to tell apart from the next, while the whole page still read as one identity. A lot of my time went into separating the sections without breaking that.
